iPhone, iPad and Mac

One app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Use it on the device you have – or on several, with your palettes, viewing conditions and profiles synced through iCloud.

  • iPhone
  • iPad
  • Mac

Each device has its strength

  • iPhone

    Always with you: pick colours with the camera, measure the light and see colours in light where you stand.

  • iPad

    More room: in landscape the swatches sit beside the tools, and on large iPads your palettes are at hand.

  • Mac

    The desk: a wide window, an eyedropper for the whole screen and swatches you drag straight into other apps.

Together

  • Palettes, single colours and gradients follow you between devices through your own iCloud
  • ICC profiles and colour libraries live in the app’s folder in iCloud Drive
  • Measure the light with iPhone or iPad – and see colours in the same light on the Mac New in 1.2
  • Use iPhone as the camera on the Mac, with compensation for the light New in 1.2
  • Your view layout follows along
  • Colleagues on Windows: palettes are written as files to a folder in the cloud service you use New in 1.2
